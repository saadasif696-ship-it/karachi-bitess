## Brand & Style
This design system curates an ultra-luxury culinary atelier experience for an elite clientele frequenting Karachi’s high-fashion enclaves (Clifton, DHA Phase 6). It fuses grand Mughal palatial cues with sharp, contemporary architectural minimalism. The emotional tone evokes absolute exclusivity, heirloom craftsmanship, and sensory indulgence.

The visual style employs an Obsidian Noir and Metallic Gold aesthetic: deep void-like backdrops, illuminated metallic accents, micro-textured translucent containers, and razor-sharp typographic discipline. Visual artifacts avoid loud or vernacular kitsch, relying instead on restraint, calibrated gold leaf highlights, and generous negative space to evoke timeless gastronomic prestige.

## Colors
The palette evokes liquid metallic gold suspended over deep midnight stone.

- **Obsidian Black Canvas (`#0A0A0A`)**: Grounding base canvas that acts as an endless expanse, heightening contrast.
- **Charcoal Elevated Surface (`#141414`)**: Used for structured cards, modal dialogue panels, and culinary feature framing.
- **Royal Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: The principal anchor for primary calls to action, badges, and key icons. Linear gradients transition from Champagne Shimmer (`#F3E5AB`) at the apex down to Burnished Bronze (`#AA7C11`) at the base.
- **Crisp Ivory (`#F8F5F0`)**: Primary typography baseline, providing pristine optical clarity against charcoal surfaces.
- **Muted Champagne (`#A39E93`)**: Secondary metadata, ingredient provenance notes, nutritional cues, and unobtrusive borders.

## Typography
Typographic scale reflects formal dining protocol: measured, upright, and legibly expressive. 

Headings rely on **Playfair Display**, using high-contrast serifs to mirror antique royal charters and engraved reservation ledgers. All uppercase usage for category descriptors and section indices shifts into **Montserrat** to ensure structural stability at small optical sizes with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`). Narrative descriptions, tasting notes, and interactive states render in **Plus Jakarta Sans** for crisp, effortless legibility under ambient low-light interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column desktop framework anchored by generous margins (`4rem` outer screen gutters) ensures luxury breathing room. The layout avoids claustrophobic information density; white space acts as a design material rather than empty canvas.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at `1440px`.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Micro spacings (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) structure ingredient pairings and culinary awards. Major editorial transitions utilize `space-xl` to enforce pauses between multi-course tasting menus, bespoke private dining suites, and cellar reserves.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is articulated through calibrated gold-light dispersion and tonal surface boundaries rather than murky drop shadows:

- **Base Level (`#0A0A0A`)**: Ground canvas for broad section staging.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#141414`)**: Menu cards, reservation inputs, and carousel tiles framed with a razor-thin border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Layer 2 Floating / Flyout**: Navigation bars, drop-down concierges, and dish detail modals elevate via backdrop blur (`blur(16px)`) over an alpha-layered charcoal matrix (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.85)`), bordered with `1px solid rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.3)`.
- **Glow Accents**: Focused CTAs and active states cast a diffuse, warm halo (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).

## Shapes
To preserve an aura of architectural nobility, geometries remain disciplined and tailored. All cards, interactive states, and dialogs follow a subtle, crisp soft radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`), ensuring cards retain an engraved, tactile precision. Circular formats are reserved strictly for avatar monograms and location locator indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Imperial Gold)**: Gradient fill from `#F3E5AB` through `#D4AF37` to `#AA7C11` with dark charcoal text (`#0A0A0A`, Montserrat SemiBold, uppercase, tracked). Hover initiates an interior luminosity shift and soft halo glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary CTA (Atelier Outline)**: Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` with Crisp Ivory text (`#F8F5F0`). Hover transitions border to solid gold with background fill of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Menu Folios
Constructed over `#141414` charcoal slabs with a disciplined `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` border. On hover, the border subtly brightens to `rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.45)` with a slow, smooth transition (`400ms ease-out`). Dish imagery uses a subtle dark gradient vignette at the base to seamlessly merge with the card surface.

### Chips & Badges (Dietary & Heritage)
Compact, low-profile badges using Montserrat Label typography. Rendered in dark charcoal fills with subtle gold borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`) and champagne text (`#F3E5AB`), denoting distinctions such as "Dum Pukht Classic", "Wagyu Nihari", or "Clifton Exclusive".

### Form Controls & Date Selectors
Minimalist underline or enclosed charcoal inputs with inset padding (`0.75rem 1rem`). Placeholder text rendered in Muted Champagne (`#A39E93`). Focus state swaps borders to solid Royal Metallic Gold with zero native browser outlines.

### Specialized Component: Location Selector
A refined segment controller toggling between flagship branches (Clifton, DHA Phase 6, Tariq Road). Active branch features an illuminated gold underline bar with high-contrast Ivory text, while inactive branches rest in Muted Champagne.
